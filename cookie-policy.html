Cookie Policy

Last updated October 3, 2026
Kertory-JEPA — CEO & Founder: Vikash Kumar

1. The short version

This Site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or any third-party tracking. It's a small research project page, not a product with a marketing funnel. The only things that resemble "cookies" here come from sign-in and a couple of on-page conveniences, described below.

2. What we use, specifically

WhatPurposeSet by
Firebase Authentication sessionKeeps you signed in (Google, GitHub, or admin) across page reloads, so you don't have to sign in again every time.Google Firebase
Google / GitHub sign-in cookiesUsed by Google/GitHub themselves during the sign-in popup, under their own cookie policies.Google / GitHub
Google FontsLoads the Site's typefaces (Inter, JetBrains Mono) from Google's font servers.Google Fonts

We don't set any cookies ourselves for analytics, advertising, or tracking your activity across other sites.

3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings at any time. Doing so may sign you out of Google/GitHub sign-in on this Site, but every other part of the Site — the research, results, and demo — works the same either way, since none of it depends on cookies.

4. Changes to this policy

If what the Site uses changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the "Send feedback" link in the Site's Resources section, or directly by email.
