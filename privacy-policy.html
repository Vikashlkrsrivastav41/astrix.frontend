1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information this Site collects, why, and how it's handled. This Site is a small, independent research project — it does not run ads, does not use analytics or tracking cookies, and does not sell personal information to anyone.

2. Information we collect

The Site only collects information you actively provide, in these specific places:

WhereWhat's collected
Signing in (Google / GitHub)Your name and email address, as shared by Google/GitHub when you choose to sign in.
Job application formYour name, email, the role you're applying to, and your message.
Commercial license request formYour name, email, company (optional), and description of intended use.
Admin sign-inAn email/password used only by the project's own administrator — not collected from visitors.

We don't use cookies, pixels, or scripts to track your browsing behavior, and we don't collect information from the Site's read-only pages (research, results, demo) unless you choose to sign in or submit a form.

3. How we use it

  • To review and respond to job applications.
  • To review and respond to commercial license requests.
  • To let a signed-in visitor apply to a posted role.
  • To let the project's administrator manage the Site's content (uploading photos/videos, posting roles).

We don't use your information for advertising, don't build behavioral profiles, and don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Where it's stored

Submitted information is stored using Google's Firebase platform (Firestore database and Firebase Authentication), which runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. Google acts as our data processor for this storage; their own privacy and security practices apply to how the underlying infrastructure is run.

5. Sharing

We don't sell personal information. It's visible only to the Site's administrator (for reviewing applications/requests) and to Google/GitHub (as the sign-in provider you chose). We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Retention

Job applications and license requests are kept until reviewed and are deleted by the administrator afterward at their discretion. You can ask for your submission to be deleted sooner — see "Your rights" below.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete any information you've submitted (a job application, a license request, your sign-in record) by emailing the address in the Contact section below. We'll act on verified requests as soon as reasonably possible.

8. Children's privacy

This Site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site's features change. The "Updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the "Send feedback" link in the Site's Resources section, or email directly.
